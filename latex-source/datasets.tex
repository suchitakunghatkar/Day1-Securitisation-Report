\section{Dataset Overview}

The project provides four datasets that represent different stages of the securitisation lifecycle. These datasets are used to analyse loan performance, delinquency trends, portfolio behaviour, and expected losses. Together, they provide the foundation for building analytical dashboards and risk assessment models.

\vspace{0.4cm}

\subsection*{Overview of Provided Datasets}

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.5}

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|p{4cm}|X|X|}
	\hline
	\rowcolor{TableHeader}
	\textbf{Dataset} & \textbf{Purpose} & \textbf{Key Information} \\
	\hline
	
	\textbf{Auto Loan Pool}
	&
	Contains individual loan-level information.
	&
	Loan ID, borrower details, loan amount, interest rate, tenure, EMI, outstanding balance.
	\\
	\hline
	
	\textbf{DPD Snapshot History}
	&
	Tracks repayment delays over time.
	&
	Days Past Due (DPD), payment status, delinquency history, missed EMIs.
	\\
	\hline
	
	\textbf{Static Pool Vintage Data}
	&
	Measures portfolio performance by origination period.
	&
	Vintage month, cumulative defaults, recoveries, collection efficiency.
	\\
	\hline
	
	\textbf{Dynamic Loss Monthly Data}
	&
	Monitors monthly portfolio losses.
	&
	Charge-offs, recoveries, net loss, monthly default trends.
	\\
	\hline
	
\end{tabularx}

\subsection*{Business Importance}

\begin{itemize}
	
	\item Evaluate the quality of securitised loan pools.
	
	\item Monitor borrower repayment behaviour.
	
	\item Identify early warning signs of defaults.
	
	\item Estimate expected losses.
	
	\item Support credit rating decisions.
	
	\item Build Power BI dashboards for portfolio monitoring.
	
\end{itemize}
